Music videos and reels, cut for creators.

Atlas Cut Studios is a small editing studio. We cut music videos and short vertical reels for musicians, bands and creators, timed to the track and ready for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts.

What we do

  • Edit footage into music videos and 15-60 second reels, cut to the beat.
  • Prepare captions and cover frames for each platform.
  • Publish finished videos to a creator's own TikTok account, only after the creator connects the account, chooses the settings and presses Post.

Publishing to TikTok

Our publishing tool uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. The creator signs in with TikTok, sees a preview of the video, edits the caption, picks who can see it and which interactions to allow, declares any commercial content, and then posts. We never post without that click, and we never add a watermark. See our Privacy Policy for what TikTok data we use.
