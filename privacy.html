Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Atlas Cut Studios ("we") runs the website https://willusher1019.github.io/atlascutstudios and a publishing tool that posts videos to a creator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data we use, why, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted. Contact: will.g.usher@gmail.com.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Our host may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security, for up to 30 days.

TikTok data we use

When you connect your TikTok account with "Continue with TikTok", TikTok asks you to grant two permissions: user.info.basic and video.publish. With them we use only the following:

DataWhy we use it
Account name and nickname (display name / username) and TikTok open ID To show you which account a video will be posted to, and to tell connected accounts apart.
Profile picture (avatar) Shown next to your name on the post screen so you can confirm the account.
Creator info: the visibility options your account allows, whether comments, duets and stitches are turned off, and the longest video you can post To build the post screen with only the choices TikTok allows for your account, and to stop a post that TikTok would refuse.
Post status and post ID To tell you whether the upload is still processing, has been published, or failed.
Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok To act on your instruction to post. They are stored securely and never shared.

We do not read your followers, messages, likes, other videos or analytics. We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train any model. The video and caption you post are sent to TikTok only when you press Post.

How long we keep it

  • Creator info and avatar: fetched fresh each time you open the post screen and not stored afterwards.
  • Account name, open ID and tokens: kept while your account is connected; deleted within 30 days of you disconnecting or asking us to delete them.
  • Post IDs and status: kept for up to 12 months as a record of what was published, then deleted.

Deleting your data

Email will.g.usher@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your TikTok username. We will delete everything we hold about your account within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also revoke our access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (wording may vary by app version); we then stop using your data and delete it as above.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new date.
